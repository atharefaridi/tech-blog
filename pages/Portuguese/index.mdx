### **Week 1: Build a Foundation**
**Goal**: Learn pronunciation, basic vocabulary, and simple grammar.

1. **Master Pronunciation (Days 1-2)**  
   - **Why**: European Portuguese has unique sounds, like nasal vowels (e.g., "pão") and distinct intonation, which differ from Brazilian Portuguese.
   - **How to Use Grok**:
     - Ask me: “How do I pronounce common European Portuguese sounds like nasal vowels or the ‘r’ sound?” I’ll provide detailed explanations and examples.
     - Request phonetic spellings for tricky words (e.g., “pão” as [pɐ̃w̃]). Practice repeating after my descriptions.
     - Ask for a list of 10 common words with audio-like descriptions (e.g., “Describe how to say ‘obrigado’ and ‘bom dia’ in European Portuguese”).
   - **Action**: Spend 30 minutes daily practicing pronunciation. Record yourself and compare to native speaker audio (available on apps like Practice Portuguese).[](https://www.practiceportuguese.com/)

2. **Learn Essential Vocabulary (Days 3-5)**  
   - **Why**: Focus on high-frequency words for greetings, numbers, and daily interactions.
   - **How to Use Grok**:
     - Ask: “Give me a list of 50 essential European Portuguese words for beginners, like greetings, numbers, and basic verbs, with translations and pronunciations.”
     - Request flashcards: “Create a table of 10 common phrases (e.g., ‘How are you?’) in European Portuguese with English translations.”
     - Practice with me by asking: “Quiz me on basic vocabulary like numbers and greetings in European Portuguese.”
   - **Action**: Aim for 20-30 words daily. Use spaced repetition (reviewing at increasing intervals) to retain them. Apps like Memrise or Practice Portuguese’s Smart Review can complement this.[](https://www.practiceportuguese.com/)[](https://www.memrise.com/en/learn-portuguese)[](https://www.portugalresident.com/a-smarter-approach-to-learning-european-portuguese/)

3. **Grasp Basic Grammar (Days 6-7)**  
   - **Why**: Understand sentence structure and verb conjugation basics (e.g., present tense of regular verbs like “falar”).
   - **How to Use Grok**:
     - Ask: “Explain the present tense conjugation of regular verbs in European Portuguese with examples.”
     - Request simple sentence construction: “Show me how to form basic sentences like ‘I am happy’ or ‘She eats bread’ in European Portuguese.”
     - Practice: “Give me 5 fill-in-the-blank sentences to practice verb conjugation in European Portuguese.”
